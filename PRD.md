# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Project Overview
**Working Title:** Astral Resonance  
**Genre:** Music-Driven Immersive Explorer / Action-Platformer  
**Core Premise:** A 3D atmospheric game where the world is procedurally generated and driven by the music. The player experiences an "out-of-body" astral projection sensation, navigating a deep, tribal, and mystical world that physically reacts to the beats, polyrhythms, and frequencies of the selected audio track.

## 2. Core Gameplay & Mechanics
### 2.1 The "Out-of-Body" Sensation
The game centers on the duality of physical weight and ethereal freedom, heavily relying on crisp movement and fast, high-sensitivity mouse control.
*   **Astral Snapping (Flick-Dash):** The physical body is grounded and heavy. The player can project their astral form forward by executing highly precise, fast flicks to target floating geometries or Resonance Points. Once the astral form reaches the target, the physical body is pulled to it with a rhythmic shockwave. This rewards high-sensitivity precision and rapid target acquisition.
*   **Realm Shifting:** The player can detach from the physical realm (dark, stone, monolithic) into the astral realm (luminous, zero-gravity) during specific sections of a track (e.g., atmospheric bridges or vocal sections). 
*   **Ethereal Momentum:** During massive musical drops or heavy breakdowns, gravity is temporarily disabled. The player surfs the audio waves, utilizing the momentum generated by the environment's displacement.

### 2.2 The Game Loop
1.  **Track Selection:** The player selects an audio track.
2.  **Procedural Generation:** The game generates a unique environment seed based on the track's audio profile.
3.  **Traversal & Ritual:** The player navigates the shifting landscape to find and activate "Resonance Points" before the track ends.
4.  **Survival:** During heavy rhythmic sections, the terrain shatters or shifts violently, requiring rapid Astral Snapping to survive.

## 3. Art & Audio Direction
### 3.1 Visual Style
*   **Aesthetic:** Deep, tribal, mystical. Ancient nature mixed with unknown magic.
*   **Color Palette:** Obsidian black, deep moss greens, and ash, contrasted with luminescent ritual patterns (neon orange, deep blue, crimson). 
*   **Player Trail:** Precise movements leave geometric light trails that fade in sync with the BPM.

### 3.2 Audio & Atmosphere
The game thrives on complex, atmospheric, and heavy sounds. The world generation and mechanics are optimized for tracks featuring heavy polyrhythms, atmospheric tension, and dynamic range.
*   **Musical Vibe:** The procedural generation algorithms should be tuned to handle the complex rhythmic structures, deep bass, and soaring atmospheric transitions characteristic of progressive metal, alternative rock, and nu-metal (e.g., Tool, Korn, Deftones, Gojira, Lucid Planet, Humanity's Last Breath).

## 4. Technical Architecture
To achieve a highly responsive, browser-accessible, and easily maintainable structure, the game will utilize a modern web-based 3D stack alongside a robust backend environment.

### 4.1 Frontend & 3D Engine
*   **Engine:** React Three Fiber (R3F) for declarative 3D rendering.
*   **Audio Processing:** Web Audio API (Fast Fourier Transform - FFT) for real-time frequency and BPM analysis.
    *   *Lows (Kick/Bass):* Controls displacement maps (ground tremors, monolith expansion).
    *   *Mids (Guitars/Vocals):* Controls bloom, emission of ancient runes, and lighting intensity.
    *   *Highs (Cymbals):* Drives particle system emissions.

### 4.2 Backend & Infrastructure
*   **API / Backend:** Laravel (PHP 8.4) to handle player profiles, high scores, custom track metadata, and backend logic.
*   **Local Development:** Docker Compose to seamlessly orchestrate the Laravel backend, database, and frontend environments.
*   **Quality Assurance:** Playwright for End-to-End (E2E) testing of UI components, user flows, and canvas initialization.

## 5. Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Scope
1.  **Audio Analyzer:** A working FFT script that correctly separates a chosen track into Low, Mid, and High frequencies in real-time.
2.  **Basic Environment:** A flat R3F plane with primitive monolithic structures that scale/displace based on the low-frequency data.
3.  **Astral Snapping:** Implementation of the core movement mechanic—aiming at a target and "snapping" the camera/player object to it seamlessly.
4.  **Post-Processing:** Basic bloom and chromatic aberration tied to audio peaks to sell the "out-of-body" impact.
